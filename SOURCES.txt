Bank transfer numbers
  Not present in static HTML. js/payments.js is reused as-is from the previous
  release: it assembles two account numbers at runtime from split, XOR-obfuscated
  digit groups, and writes them to the DOM only after "Show bank details".
  No IBAN prefix, SWIFT code, bank name or account holder name is published.
