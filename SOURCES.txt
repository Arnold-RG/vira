Bank transfer
  File:     assets/payments/bank.svg
  Original generic bank pictogram drawn for this site. Not a brand mark.

Bank transfer numbers
  Not present in static HTML. A first-party module (js/payments.js)
  assembles two account numbers at runtime from split, XOR-obfuscated
  digit groups, and writes them to the DOM only after the visitor
  clicks "Show bank details". No IBAN prefix, SWIFT code, bank name
  or account holder name is published (none were in the source data).
