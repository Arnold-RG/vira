      About Vira

      What is Vira?

      Vira is a technology and business-support company. We build websites, apps and games, set up networks and connectivity, create AI video content, and help people manage and grow their business.

      What does Vira offer?

      Websites, apps, games, networking, internet provision and connectivity, tech support, AI creation (AI videos, AI teaching, video content), business management, freelance support, and financial support and management.

      Who is Vira for?

      Individuals, freelancers, founders, small businesses and larger companies in Rwanda who want technology that works without the stress.

      Who is behind Vira?

      Vira was founded by Arnold Rurangwa, Founder & CEO.

      Who do you work with?

      Vira works only with businesses and clients in Rwanda.

      Getting started

      How do I start a project?

      Use the project builder or the contact form: pick a service and a plan, add your details, and send it straight to us on WhatsApp.

      What do you need from me?

      Your goals, any content you have (logo, text, photos) and someone to give feedback on the prototype.

      How does your process work?

      Structure, prototype, share and discuss, refine, your approval, then build and go live.

      Business sites, portfolios, landing pages and online shops, all mobile-friendly with light and dark modes.

      Networking: what networking help do you offer?

      Help with computers, software, accounts, websites and everyday tech issues, remotely.

      AI creation: what do you make with AI?

      AI videos, video content for brands and social media, and teaching you how to use AI tools yourself.

      Can you teach me or my team AI?

      Yes, simple, practical AI lessons tailored to your work.

      How does Vira help my business grow?

      We give you a professional online presence, organise your processes, help with business management and content, so you can focus on customers.

      What is business management support?

      Help organising tasks, tools, schedules and workflows so your business runs smoothly.

      Yes, we help you set up your online presence, tools and workflow to get started.

      Working with Vira

      Do you collaborate with other founders or enterprises?

      Yes. We work with founders, small businesses and larger teams in Rwanda, and partner on shared projects.

      Can Vira work with my existing engineering team?

      Pricing and payment

      How much does it cost?

      We have four plans for businesses in Rwanda: Basic, 250 PLN, with 7 days of support and a 3-week guarantee after publishing. Standard, 500 PLN, with 15 days of support and a 6-week guarantee. Super, 700 PLN, with 21 days of support and a 2.5-month guarantee. Plus, 1000 PLN, with full care and a 4-month guarantee. Every plan can be paid in two halves: before the prototype and after you approve it. If you want your own .rw domain (like yourbusiness.rw) to publish your project, add 500 PLN on Basic, 250 PLN on Standard or 50 PLN on Super. On Plus, the domain is included. Every plan also has two monthly charges after launch: a 100 PLN subscription, which covers your domain renewal, the running subscriptions your project needs, and keeping one project active on social media to help it grow, and a separate service fee of 155–175 PLN per month. That makes 255–275 PLN per month in total. Custom projects get a clear quote first.

      Do I need to pay extra for a domain?

      If you want your project published on your own domain (like yourbusiness.rw) or as a live mobile app, the add-on depends on your plan: 500 PLN on Basic, 250 PLN on Standard, 50 PLN on Super. On Plus, the domain is included at no extra cost. The yearly renewal after that is covered by your 100 PLN monthly fee.

      Is there a monthly fee?

      Yes, there are two separate monthly charges after launch, 255–275 PLN in total.

      • 100 PLN subscription: every plan pays this. This covers your yearly domain renewal and all the running subscriptions your project needs, such as hosting and services. We pay these for you and take full responsibility for keeping them paid. For one project, it also covers keeping it active on your social media and working to grow its users. We can't guarantee exact user numbers, but we'll share regular updates on how it's doing.
      • Service fee, 155–175 PLN: 155 to 175 PLN per month on every plan, for our ongoing service. The exact amount is agreed with you before you start.
      What guarantee do the 250 and 500 PLN plans have?

      After your project is published, you can report a problem and we fix it free: within 3 weeks (21 days) on the 250 PLN Basic plan, and within 6 weeks on the 500 PLN Standard plan. After that, fixes are charged as extra work, and you agree the price before we start.

      What does the 2.5-month guarantee cover (700 PLN Super plan)?

      For 2 and a half months after launch, you can report any crash or fault in the work we delivered and we fix it at no extra cost. Problems caused by outside changes, such as edits by someone else or a third-party service going down, are quoted separately.

      What does the 1000 PLN Plus plan include?

      Plus is our highest level of care: you pay more, you get more. You can pay in full or in two halves: 500 PLN before prototype work starts, and 500 PLN after we show you the prototype and you give the go-ahead. In return, you get a 4-month guarantee: for 4 months after launch, we take full responsibility for fixing crashes and problems with what we've built and manage for you, at no extra cost. We also take over the day-to-day management of your business Gmail, website and social media accounts. You always stay the owner of your accounts and can remove our access at any time. Plus is also the only plan with a refund after launch: within 7 days of your project going live, you can ask for a refund and we'll return 250 PLN of the 1000 PLN.

      How can I pay?

      BLIK or MTN Mobile Money. On every plan, you can pay in full or in two halves: the first half before the prototype, and the second half after you approve it.

      Do I pay upfront?

      Not all of it. Every plan can be paid in two halves. You pay the first half before prototype work starts: 125 PLN on Basic, 250 PLN on Standard, 350 PLN on Super, or 500 PLN on Plus. You pay the second half after we show you the prototype and you give the go-ahead, and then we continue building. You can also pay in full at the start if you prefer.

      Agreements, changes and refunds

      Do you sign an NDA or contract?

      Yes, every project starts with a written agreement, and we can sign your NDA or provide ours.

      How does Vira protect security and confidentiality?

      We keep your data private, limit access to the people who need it, use secure accounts and hosting, and can sign an NDA.

      What if I want changes after approval?

      Small tweaks are included; bigger changes are quoted before we do them.

      Can I cancel?

      Yes. You get a full refund within 5 days of paying if prototype work hasn't started. After that, we deduct for work done and refund the rest. If you stop after seeing the prototype, you get part of the first half back and don't pay the second half. There is no refund once the project is live and public, except on Plus, where you can still get 250 PLN back if you ask within 7 days of launch. Final terms are in your contract.
