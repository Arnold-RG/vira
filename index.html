Who we are

A small studio with care built into every release.

Vira is a technology studio built around a simple idea: a clear human vision, supported by disciplined tools and a careful process, can deliver work with the care of a much larger company. Every release passes a quality and security gate before it reaches you.

The name. Vira comes from the Sanskrit vīra (hero, brave, strong) and echoes the Latin vir and virtus (courage, excellence, integrity, the root of “virtue”). It also stands for Vision, Innovation, Reliability and Automation: bold, honest technology with real strength behind it.

Our Mission

To give individuals, small businesses and communities intelligent digital tools that are fast, secure and genuinely useful.

Our Vision

A future where any ambitious idea, however small its budget, can have world-class technology behind it.

Our Motto

Think boldly. Build carefully. Shine together.

Our story

Built from curiosity, shaped by discipline.

Vira began with a simple question: what if a small studio could run with the structure and care of a full team? Today we work across websites, apps, games, networking, internet setup, tech support and AI creation, and we also help with business operations, video content, freelance workflows and financial systems. We serve clients wherever they are.

We keep our promises small and our standards high: honest timelines, real results and nothing published without the client's approval.

What we do

Services for the full journey

Build, connect and grow — one studio for the technology and support a business actually needs, including custom requests.

Websites & web systems

Modern business and personal websites, plus custom web apps and dashboards. Built for speed, phones and search, with a quality and security check on every release.

Mobile & desktop apps

Applications for phones, tablets and desktops, from the first prototype to a stable release. Designed around how people will actually use them, then tested before they ship.

Games

Game development and interactive experiences for the web and desktop. We shape the play, build the systems behind it and polish how it feels to use.

Networking & connectivity

Network design, setup and security for homes, offices and events, including Wi-Fi, LAN and WAN. We plan the connection, set up the hardware and leave you with a link that stays protected.

Tech support

IT helpdesk, device setup, troubleshooting and ongoing maintenance. Clear help when something breaks, and routine care so it happens less often.

AI creation

Custom AI assistants, chatbots, automation and data or machine-learning work. Built around your real workflow, then checked before you rely on it.

Video content

Planning, filming support, editing and packaging for social and web video. From a clear brief to a finished piece ready to publish.

Business & finance support

Planning, operations and reporting for small businesses, plus practical tools for tracking money and organising the books. Setup and support — not financial or tax advice.

Freelance support

Practical help for independent professionals: a clear online presence, simple workflows and the tools that keep client work moving.
