02 — Services

What we can take on

  • Websites

    A clear site for your business or a first online presence, designed and built to an agreed brief.

  • Apps

    Applications for phones, tablets and desktop computers, built for a real task you already have.

  • Games

    Games and other interactive work for the web and for desktop.

  • Networking

    Wi-Fi and wired networks for homes, offices and events, designed, set up and secured.

  • Internet provision and connectivity

    The line, the router and the Wi-Fi — a home or office that is actually online.

  • Tech support

    Help when a device or system breaks, and looking after it afterwards if you need us to.

  • AI creation

    A small, practical AI tool around a real task — an assistant, a chatbot, or a bit of automation — tested before you use it.

  • Business management

    The day-to-day systems a small business uses to run the work, set up and made clear.

  • Video content creation

    Help to plan, film, edit and package video for a website or for social channels.

  • Freelance support

    An online presence and the simple tools that keep an independent worker’s client work moving.

  • Financial support and management

    Practical tools for tracking money and organising the books. This is setup and support, not financial or tax advice.
